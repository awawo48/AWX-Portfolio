AWX Visual

Video Editor & Filmmaker

I'm a passionate video editor and filmmaker based in Kelantan, Malaysia, with a keen eye for storytelling through visual media. My journey began with a love for cinema and has evolved into a professional career crafting compelling video content.

Specializing in video editing, color grading, and cinematography, I bring stories to life using industry-standard tools like DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Premiere Pro, and After Effects.
